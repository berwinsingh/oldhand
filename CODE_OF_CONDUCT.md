# Code of Conduct

Oldhand welcomes useful, respectful participation.

## Expected

- Be clear, kind, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and remove secrets or private project data from reports.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and project direction.

## Not accepted

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private information are not accepted in repository spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

For a private conduct report, use the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/berwinsingh/oldhand/security/advisories/new) and prefix the title with `[Conduct]`. Reports will be reviewed by the maintainer and kept private where possible.
